The stack

Six specialists,
one shared mind.

Each agent owns its domain completely. Each one builds on what the others produce through a shared memory store. Together, they form a founding team that operates around the clock.

01
Legal

Files the company.

Forms Delaware LLCs via Computer Use. Pulls the EIN from IRS.gov. Drafts founder agreements, NDAs, IP assignment, and vesting schedules.

02
Web

Ships the site.

Generates copy, layout, and the waitlist flow from your one-paragraph pitch. Deploys live via Framer or Vercel before the end of day two. Iterates from plain-English feedback.

03
Research

Validates the market.

Full TAM/SAM/SOM, competitor landscape, customer personas, pricing benchmarks, and a go/no-go summary. Real-time Google grounding — not training-cutoff data.

04
Marketing

Finds first customers.

Builds the GTM plan, writes the cold email sequence, runs Google and Meta ads within your budget, and generates social content. Adjusts autonomously on what converts.

05
Technical

Builds on demand.

Describe a feature in plain English. The Technical Agent writes the spec, generates the code, reviews itself, and deploys. You see the result — never the code.

06
Ops

Runs everything else.

Manages your task list, surfaces what needs a human decision, sends the weekly digest, drafts routine email replies, and remembers every decision the company has ever made.

The shared mind

Not six chatbots.
One company.

Every agent reads from and writes to the same vector store — every decision, document, campaign, and conversation lives in one persistent memory. When Marketing writes a cold email, it knows the personas Research surfaced. When Technical ships a feature, Ops files it in the weekly digest. When Legal redlines a contract, the others learn from the change.

After ninety days, Astra knows your company better than any human consultant could — and that memory belongs to you.
