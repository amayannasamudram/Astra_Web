FAQ

Honest answers.

What's included in the one-time $249 setup?
Everything that turns an idea into an entity on day one: the Delaware LLC or C-Corp filing, the EIN from the IRS, founder agreements and NDAs in your specific context, IP assignment documents, the full first market-research report, and a live landing page deployed to a real domain. It is charged once at signup and never again.
Is the LLC actually filed, or is this a template generator?
Actually filed. The Legal Agent uses Computer Use on Stripe Atlas to navigate the real filing UI, complete payment, and submit the formation documents. The EIN is then retrieved from IRS.gov the same way. You receive the real certificate of formation and EIN letter from the State of Delaware.
How does yearly billing work?
Yearly is billed once for the year and saves you 30% versus monthly. You can switch back to monthly at renewal. Mid-year cancellation refunds the remaining unused months on a pro-rata basis.
Is this legal advice?
No. Astra prepares documents and files paperwork autonomously, but every output ships with an explicit disclaimer: AI-generated document preparation, not legal advice. We recommend reviewing important documents with a licensed attorney before signing anything significant.
What happens to my unused credits?
They roll over indefinitely. There is no monthly expiration. If you skip a month of heavy lifting, your credit balance is still there next month.
Can I cancel?
Yes, any time. The setup fee is non-refundable once filing has begun, but Build and Scale are month-to-month with no commitment. If you cancel, you keep every document, the company entity, and your company memory — exportable on the way out.
What if I'm not in the US?
Delaware accepts founders from any country — that is one of its longstanding advantages. Stripe Atlas handles the registered agent requirement for non-US residents and the EIN is obtained whether or not you have a US Social Security Number.
Do you have an enterprise plan?
For university accelerators and incubators, yes — we offer cohort pricing starting at $1,500 per cohort of ten founders. Reach out via the contact form on the waitlist page.
