Disclosures

Effective Date: June 1, 2026  ·  Operated by: Astra LLC

This Disclosure Agreement ("Disclosure") provides required and voluntary disclosures about the nature of the Astra platform, its AI systems, its interactions with third-party services, and the limitations of its outputs. By using Astra, you acknowledge that you have read and understood these disclosures.

1. AI Agent Nature Disclosure

Astra is entirely operated by artificial intelligence systems. None of the agents in the Astra platform — Legal Agent, Technical Agent, Web Agent, Research Agent, Marketing Agent, or Ops Agent — are human professionals. You are not communicating with a licensed attorney, financial advisor, accountant, engineer, or marketing specialist when interacting with any Astra agent.

The Agent Stack is powered by a combination of large language models, code generation models, and orchestration systems. Astra may update its model stack from time to time. Material changes to the AI systems underlying the platform will be communicated via dashboard notification.

No attorney-client relationship is formed between you and Astra, or between you and any third party, by reason of any document generated on this platform.

  • Founder and co-founder agreements

  • Equity vesting schedules
  • Independent contractor agreements
  • Any other legal document produced by the Legal Agent on demand

You are solely responsible for: (a) reviewing all generated documents with a licensed attorney in your jurisdiction before execution; (b) determining whether any generated document is appropriate, enforceable, or legally sufficient for your specific situation; and (c) any consequences arising from executing or relying upon AI-generated documents without independent legal review.

  • Astra acts as your agent in completing these filings. You, as the founding principal, remain legally responsible for the accuracy of all information submitted, including business name, registered agent selection, organizer information, and intended business purpose.
  • Astra is not a registered agent and does not provide registered agent services. Entity formation through applicable third-party portals may include a registered agent selected and provided by those services.
  • Astra does not provide tax advice. Selection of entity type may have significant tax implications. You are encouraged to consult a tax professional before proceeding with entity formation.
  • Filing timelines are subject to state and federal processing times outside of Astra's control.
  • All formation fees charged by the State of Delaware, the IRS, and any third-party filing services are separate from and in addition to Astra's platform fees. These fees are charged directly by the relevant third parties.
